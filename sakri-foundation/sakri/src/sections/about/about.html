About us

A foundation built for women and children

Sakri Foundation was established on 9 June 2025 in Dharwad, Karnataka, with one belief: lasting change begins with those too often unheard.

We work at the intersection of women's rights, child protection, education, healthcare and community development — bridging the gap between awareness and action across Dharwad's neighbourhoods and schools.
